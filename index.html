Ranked worst to best

I Sold My Silver Anniversary Bracelet And Spent ₹35,000 Trying To Look Younger. Here Are All 5 Things I Tried, Ranked Worst To Best.

Four of them were expensive. The one that actually worked costs ₹499.

By Meera S., 34, Pune

Let me start with the embarrassing part.

Seven months ago, I walked into a jeweller's shop with a silver bracelet I'd been given on an anniversary and walked out with cash. I didn't need the money for an emergency. I needed it for an LED face mask.

That's how bad it had become.

I'd turned 34, and something had shifted in my face. It wasn't dramatic. There were fine lines at the corners of my eyes that stayed even after I stopped smiling. My skin looked dull by 4 pm no matter what I applied in the morning. In photos I looked tired even when I wasn't.

Then my older sister who is two years older than me got asked if she was my younger sister.

I laughed, went home and started spending.

Over the next six months I spent almost exactly ₹35,000 on five different things that promised younger, glowing skin. I kept every bill. I took a photo in the same window light every Sunday. And I'm ranking all five here, worst to best, so that you can skip straight to what works and keep your jewellery.

5

Collagen powder

₹7,200 for three months

While I was waiting for the LED mask to arrive, I ordered this because every influencer I follow drinks it.

I mixed one scoop into water every morning for 90 days. It tasted like fish that had been near a strawberry, and I drank it anyway.

My nails got a little stronger. My skin looked exactly the same. The fine lines in my week 12 photo were no different from week 1.

Maybe it works for some people over a longer time. But at ₹2,400 a month I couldn't afford to wait and see, and nothing on my face told me to continue.

VerdictExpensive habit, and no change I could see. Last place.

4

Salon hydra facials

₹9,000 for three sessions

I'll be fair to these. For about four days after each facial, my skin looked fantastic. It was plump and shiny and I felt great.

On day five it was back to normal.

That's the problem. A facial was something I rented at ₹3,000 a session, and the glow came with an expiry date. To keep it up I'd need to go every week, which is ₹12,000 a month. I'd run out of bracelets very quickly.

VerdictLovely before a function. Useless as a long-term answer.

3

Retinol serum

₹3,800 for two bottles and the extra moisturiser I needed

Retinol is the ingredient everyone recommends, and I understand why. It's well known, and plenty of people get results with it.

My skin did not cooperate.

Within two weeks my cheeks were peeling, my face stung when I applied sunscreen, and I had red patches near my nose. People call this "purging" and tell you to push through. I pushed through for nine weeks. The lines looked slightly softer, but my skin looked irritated and flaky, which isn't exactly a glow.

You also can't be casual with it. It's for night-time only, you need strict sunscreen, and it isn't advised during pregnancy. That was a lot of rules for a small result.

VerdictIt did something, but the irritation wasn't worth it for my skin. Third place.

2

LED face mask

₹14,500

This is the one the bracelet paid for.

I wore it for 15 minutes every night, lying on my bed looking like a robot. My family found it hilarious.

To be honest, after about ten weeks my skin texture did look a bit more even. It was not nothing.

But "a bit more even" for ₹14,500 hurts. So does remembering to charge it, clean it and lie still for 15 minutes every night. By month three I was using it twice a week. By month four it was in a drawer, and that's where it is now.

VerdictMild results at a painful price, and far too easy to give up on. Second place, mostly because everything below it was worse.
